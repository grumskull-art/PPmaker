These timings describe the course, not a recommended slide count or teaching
time for each slide. Exact properties, tables and regulatory thresholds require
the actual assignment and reference material.

Assessment contexts must be selected explicitly. The TM course plans describe
a question allocated 24 hours in advance, a 15-minute individual presentation
and a 20-minute oral examination. The BM4 project brief describes a 60-minute
group exam: 20 minutes presentation, 30 minutes questioning and 10 minutes
deliberation; students receive individual grades. Its report limit is six
pages per student, excluding annexes and images. The EL-TEK1 course plan
describes 30 minutes preparation and a 30-minute oral slot for the selected
EL examination. The study order states that a lottery determines EL versus TM;
consult the current exam schedule for the particular student's allocation.
Do not transfer TM or project timing into an ordinary classroom lesson.

The project brief refers to AI procedures Q-0636 and Q-0637, which have not
been supplied. This app cannot infer their permitted use, disclosure format or
the assignment's current deadline. The RKJ checklist supports formulation of
the problem and subquestions; it is not a substitute for the project brief.
